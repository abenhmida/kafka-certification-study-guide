## How to Use This Part

Recommended progression:

1. Mock Exam A — Developer Fundamentals
2. Mock Exam B — Producer / Consumer

5. Mock Exam E — Security / Networking

7. Mock Exam G — Full CCDAK simulation
8. Mock Exam H — Full CCAAK simulation

For each exam:

- answer without looking at the key
- record your score
- classify every wrong answer
- review the associated Kafka concept
- retake missed questions after 24–48 hours

Suggested score interpretation:

| Score | Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- |
| 90–100% | Excellent |
| 80–89% | Strong |
| 70–79% | Borderline — review weak domains |
| <70% | Rebuild fundamentals before attempting the full simulation |
